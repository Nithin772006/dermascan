Clinical Ethics & Safety Notice

Informational screening assistance only

DermaScan AI is an engineering research and preliminary triage tool developed using the HAM10000 academic dataset. It does not provide medical diagnoses, histological evaluations, or therapeutic recommendations. Machine learning models can produce false positives and false negatives. If you observe an irregular, growing, bleeding, or rapidly changing skin lesion, please consult a board-certified dermatologist or seek immediate clinical care.

Always confirm any finding with professional clinical examination.
