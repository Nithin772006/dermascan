Clinical Motivation

Bridging computer vision & accessible dermatology

Dermatological conditions impact over 1.9 billion individuals globally. Early detection of malignant lesions such as melanoma drastically improves patient outcomes — yet access to board-certified dermatologists is often constrained by geographic distance, specialist shortages, and cost. DermaScan AI explores how transfer-learned deep learning can offer rapid preliminary triage and personal tracking.

Medical Benchmark

The HAM10000 Dataset

"Human Against Machine with 10,000 training images" (Tschandl et al.) is a premier open-access dermatoscopic benchmark collected across multi-site clinical centers, serving as the training and validation bedrock for DermaScan AI.

Model Architecture

Transfer Learning with MobileNetV2

Leveraging ImageNet pre-trained weights allows our network to capture complex spatial gradients with reduced overfitting and rapid execution speed.

Project Outcomes

Engineering & research contributions

High-Efficiency Multiclass Classifier

Distinguishes subtle morphological differentiators between benign keratoses, melanocytic nevi, and hazardous malignancies with balanced precision and recall.

Full-Stack Telemedicine Prototype

Complete end-to-end integration: secure cookie sessions, multipart image upload streams, SQL persistence, real-time probability charts, and interactive rule-based health assistant.

Patient-Centered Triage Experience

Translates complex mathematical loss curves and tensor probabilities into readable, reassuring visual analytics designed to foster clinical partnership.

Extensible Research Architecture

Engineered for lightweight deployment across serverless edge clouds and local workstations alike with zero configuration drift.

Medical Notice

Assistive screening, not medical advice

DermaScan AI is intended strictly for educational and preliminary screening demonstration purposes. Machine learning models cannot replace dermatoscopic dermoscopy, histological biopsy, or clinical judgment. If you have concerns about skin changes or irregular lesions, please consult a qualified medical professional immediately.
